## Standing rules

Lessons from retros, kept by `soloagile rule`. Each says where it came from.

1. Before a new skill's card goes to Needs your look, run the skill once for real and say what it did on the tested: line. (From retro of M16, #139.)
2. Once a card's pictures are taken, delete its soloagile shots folders from /tmp. (From retro of M16, #141.)
3. Move a card to Doing only when its own build starts, one card at a time. (From retro of M16, #145.)
