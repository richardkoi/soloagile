## Standing rules

Lessons from retros, kept by `soloagile rule`. Each says where it came from.

1. Before a new skill's card goes to Needs your look, run the skill once for real and say what it did on the tested: line. (From retro of M16, #139.)
2. Once a card's pictures are taken, delete its soloagile shots folders from /tmp. (From retro of M16, #141.)
3. Move a card to Doing only when its own build starts, one card at a time. (From retro of M16, #145.)
4. Put a card that comes after a milestone's work is done into a new milestone, not the open old one. (From retro of M9, #162.)
5. Take a card's phone shots before it goes to Needs your look. (From retro of M17, #163.)
6. Name only projects on this machine in a milestone's done when. (From retro of M15, #165.)
7. Release a fix as a new version as soon as it is committed. (From retro of M19, #175.)
8. When the rules grow, check the session start text stays under 9,500 characters. (From retro of M18, #177.)
9. When a card makes a standing rule out of date, remove that rule in the same card. (From retro of M18, #174.)
10. Commit only when the tests pass: chain the commit on the test command's own exit code, never on a grep of its output. (From retro of M20 and M23, #186.)
11. Before measuring against a bar, write in the done when exactly what the measure counts. (From retro of M20 and M23, #179.)
